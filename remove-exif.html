What metadata removal does not do

Metadata cleanup does not redact visible faces, addresses, documents, street signs, reflections or landmarks in the pixels. It also does not prove who created a photo or where it originated. Review supported formats and limitations before relying on the result.

EXIF removal questions

Does removing EXIF reduce JPEG image quality?

The current supported JPEG cleaner removes targeted metadata segments while copying the encoded image data rather than decoding and recompressing the picture.

Can EXIF contain a photo's location?

Yes, some files contain GPS coordinates, but many do not. Whether GPS exists depends on the device, settings and processing history of the specific file.

Can I view metadata without removing it?

Yes. Use the MetWipe EXIF viewer to inspect supported fields before deciding whether to clean the file.

Does removing metadata hide visible location clues?

No. Metadata and visible image content are different. Signs, landmarks and addresses visible in the image require separate editing or redaction.
