Photo metadata: what your image may reveal

A JPEG can carry technical and identifying context that is invisible when you simply look at the picture.

Device and software

Manufacturer, model and software tags can reveal which camera, phone or editing program produced or modified a file.

Dates and camera settings

Capture and modification timestamps can appear alongside ISO, aperture, exposure and lens information.

Privacy is broader than metadata

Removing metadata does not hide information visible in the image itself, such as faces, addresses, documents, reflections or landmarks.
