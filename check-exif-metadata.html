Check EXIF metadata in a photo

A photo can look harmless while still carrying camera, time and location information inside its file metadata.

Quick answer

EXIF and related photo metadata can include camera make/model, capture settings, timestamps and—when present—GPS coordinates. MetWipe lets you inspect supported photo metadata locally before deciding whether a cleaned copy is needed.

Why this specific task matters

Metadata and pixels are different privacy surfaces

EXIF cleanup cannot hide a street sign, face, screen, badge or address visible in the image itself.

Location is optional but high impact

Not every photo contains GPS coordinates, which is why checking before sharing is useful.

Timestamps can matter even without GPS

A capture date can reveal sensitive timing even when no coordinates are stored.

Safe workflow

  1. Select the photo you plan to share.
  2. Review the supported EXIF, GPS and related metadata fields.
  3. Create a cleaned copy when those fields are unnecessary.
  4. Verify the output and inspect the visible pixels for private information before posting.

Limits to understand

The tool does not perform visual redaction and may not expose every proprietary MakerNote or vendor-specific field. Review the supported-format limits and the visible image separately.
