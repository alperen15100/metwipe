WORD · COMPANY

Remove Company metadata from a DOCX file

Word files can carry organization-related application properties even when the company name never appears in the visible document.

Quick answer

The DOCX package can include application properties such as Company and Manager, plus custom properties with internal labels. MetWipe inspects supported OOXML properties, creates a cleaned copy and verifies the output locally.

Organization data that may travel with a DOCX

Field / areaWhat it containsWhy review it
CompanyApplication propertyCan identify the organization associated with the document.
ManagerApplication propertyMay reveal an internal role or reporting context.
Custom propertiesOrganization-defined key/value fieldsCan contain project names, department codes or workflow labels.
Visible brandingLogos, letterheads and typed company namesThese are document content and remain visible after metadata cleanup.

Why this specific task matters

Company metadata can exist without visible branding

A plain-looking document can still carry organization fields inside the package.

Custom properties can be more revealing than defaults

Internal document-management systems sometimes add their own metadata keys.

Share a purpose-built copy

If the original properties are useful internally, keep the original and distribute the cleaned copy instead.

Safe workflow

  1. Open the DOCX tool and select the distribution copy.
  2. Inspect Company, Manager and custom properties.
  3. Create a cleaned output if those values should not leave your organization.
  4. Verify the rebuilt DOCX and inspect visible branding, comments and embedded content separately.

Limits to understand

Cleaning Company-related properties does not remove logos, letterheads, typed organization names, macros, embedded documents or other visible content.
