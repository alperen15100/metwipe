CAMERA METADATA PRIVACY

Remove camera information from a photo

JPEG EXIF can contain camera or phone model, lens and exposure settings, timestamps, software information and sometimes GPS coordinates. MetWipe removes supported JPEG metadata segments while creating a separate cleaned copy.

OPEN JPEG EXIF REMOVER

What camera information can be stored?

Depending on the device and file, EXIF may include manufacturer, model, lens, ISO, exposure time, aperture, capture date and GPS fields. Files differ, so inspect the individual image first.

Remove supported metadata

  1. Inspect the JPEG with the EXIF viewer.
  2. Note any device, time or GPS fields you do not want to share.
  3. Run the supported JPEG cleaner.
  4. Download the new copy rather than overwriting your original.
  5. Re-open the cleaned copy in the viewer to verify the result.

No extra JPEG recompression pass

The current JPEG cleaner targets supported metadata application and comment segments while copying the encoded image data itself. See the supported-formats page for exact behavior and limitations.

What this does not remove

A metadata cleaner does not alter visible details in the photograph. Logos, faces, addresses, documents and location clues visible in pixels require separate editing or redaction.
