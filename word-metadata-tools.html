Why focused Word pages exist

“Remove metadata from Word,” “remove Author,” “remove Last Modified By” and “remove Company metadata” describe related but different user tasks. MetWipe keeps one broad DOCX cleaner as the main transactional page while focused pages explain each property and route back to the maintained tool.
